Let's make a game!
  name:Lossland
  author:Icysarcasm
Settings
  building cost increase:110%
  building cost refund: 50%
Layout
  use default
Buttons
  *woodbutton
  desc: Scavenge Wood
  on click: yield 1 Wood
  on click: 
      if (chance(25%)) yield 1 Stone
  on click: 
      if (have stoneaxe and chance(15%)) yield 1 Blood
  *huntbutton
  desc: Hunt animals for Hide
  on click: yield 1 Hide
  req: have (stoneaxe)
Resources 
  *Wood
  desc: Scraps of wood, I can work with these.
  *Stone
  desc: Small Stones, Usable.
  hidden when 0
  *Blood
  desc: Mine, or others. Doesn't matter.
  hidden when 0
  *Hide
  desc: Animal Hide, good quality.
  hidden when 0 
Upgrades
 *stoneaxe
  name: Stone Axe
  desc: Suprisingly Sharp, Doubles Wood gained from Scavenging
  cost: 2 Stone
  cost: 10 Wood
  passive: multiply yield of woodButton by 2
  req: 1 Stone earned
*firstblood
  name: First Blood
  desc: An ancient prayer for good harvests. Multiply Wood scavenged by 4
  cost: 1 Blood
  req: 1 Blood earned
  passive: multiply yield of woodButton by 4
